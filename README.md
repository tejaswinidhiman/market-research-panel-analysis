# Market Research Panel Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end market research and panel analysis project focused on the potato processing industry.

The project involved transforming survey and panel data into structured business insights using Microsoft Excel, Power BI, DAX, and data visualization techniques.

The analysis covers market and operational aspects such as equipment requirements, production volume, electronic sorter adoption, brands and models, pricing, export/domestic operations, and decision-making factors.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze survey responses and panel data
- Clean, merge, and structure research data using Excel
- Identify patterns across countries, organizations, and business roles
- Analyze equipment usage, capacity, pricing, and investment expectations
- Develop interactive Power BI dashboards
- Use DAX for analytical calculations and business metrics
- Present findings through clear and actionable visualizations

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, preparation, merging and analysis
- Power BI – Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- DAX – Measures and analytical calculations
- Market Research – Questionnaire and panel-data analysis
- Data Visualization – Business-oriented charts and dashboards
